TOWER PROTOCOL

⚔️ ASCENT MODE

Deploy operatives floor by floor to scale the tower.

SQUAD ASSEMBLY

  • YOUR DECK: 10 Operatives — 2 ELITES (30P) · 4 VETERANS (20P) · 4 RECRUITS (10P)
  • STARTING HAND: 5 Cards (1 Elite · 2 Vets · 2 Recruits)

COMBAT PROTOCOLS

  • EACH FLOOR: Select one operative to deploy. They are permanently consumed.
  • A random card from your reserve instantly fills their slot.
  • CLASH PHASE: Some floors trigger a typing battle — name operatives matching the displayed tag before SHADOW.AI does. First to 3 hits wins the floor.
  • Your card's power level determines auto-resolve outcomes — Elites overpower Recruits, but never underestimate the Shadow.

♾️ SURVIVAL MODE — THE MAINFRAME

Draft 3×ACE (30PT) · 3×CAPTAIN (20PT) · 3×CREW (10PT). Floors are infinite. Survive as long as possible.

OPERATIVE CLASSES

  • ACE (30PT): +3 seconds added to your turn timer on deployment.
  • CAPTAIN (20PT): Earns an extra cooldown reduction on floor clear.
  • CREW (10PT): Zero cooldown — reusable every floor.

COMBAT RULES

  • A coin flip decides who goes first each floor. Turns strictly alternate.
  • 10 seconds per turn (6s during BLITZ, +3s if ACE is deployed).
  • Get 3 correct answers to drain Shadow AI HP and clear the floor.
  • Consecutive correct answers build a streak multiplier — each hit deals more damage.
  • Wrong answer or timeout = HP damage to you only. AI answers never hurt you.
  • Floor clear = +15% HP restored.

⚡ SECTOR EVENTS — Every 5 Floors

  • 🔒 LOCKDOWN: Characters from the previous sector are banned.
  • ⚡ SURGE: Your first correct answer deals double integrity damage.
  • 💨 BLITZ: Timer cut to 6s — but every correct answer restores 5% HP.

Run ends at 0% HP. Post-run breakdown shows total correct, longest streak, fastest answer, and top operative.

🎭 VEIL PIERCER — THE IMPOSTER

Your character must match exactly ONE criterion (Row OR Col), but FAIL the other. Matches both = Strike. Fails both = Strike.
