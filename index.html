REVERSE PROTOCOL

⚠️ WARNING: SENSORY OVERLOAD IMMINENT.

🕒 THE CLOCK: You have exactly 5:00 minutes. If the clock hits 0:00, the mission fails and you score zero points.

🧠 THE FLIP: Grid clues and search results are spelled completely backwards. The difficulty levels on the main screen are also flipped (Level 1 is now Hardcore. Level 10 is Easy).

❌ PENALTIES: Strikes cost -5 seconds. Burns cost -60 seconds.

💎 THE PAYOUT

If you survive and clear the board before time runs out, the Hardcore levels (1 & 2) grant a massive 7x Multiplier! All other levels match Standard Ops.

TOWER PROTOCOL

⚔️ ASCENT MODE

Deploy operatives floor by floor to scale the tower.

SQUAD ASSEMBLY

  • YOUR DECK: 10 Operatives — 2 ELITES (30P) · 4 VETERANS (20P) · 4 RECRUITS (10P)
  • STARTING HAND: 5 Cards (1 Elite · 2 Vets · 2 Recruits)

COMBAT PROTOCOLS

  • EACH FLOOR: Select one operative to deploy. They are permanently consumed.
  • A random card from your reserve instantly fills their slot.
  • CLASH PHASE: Some floors trigger a typing battle — name operatives matching the displayed tag before SHADOW.AI does. First to 3 hits wins the floor.
  • Your card's power level determines auto-resolve outcomes — Elites overpower Recruits, but never underestimate the Shadow.

♾️ SURVIVAL MODE — THE MAINFRAME

Draft 3×ACE (30PT) · 3×CAPTAIN (20PT) · 3×CREW (10PT). Floors are infinite. Survive as long as possible.

OPERATIVE CLASSES

  • ACE (30PT): +3 seconds added to your turn timer on deployment.
  • CAPTAIN (20PT): Earns an extra cooldown reduction on floor clear.
  • CREW (10PT): Zero cooldown — reusable every floor.

COMBAT RULES

  • A coin flip decides who goes first each floor. Turns strictly alternate.
  • 10 seconds per turn (6s during BLITZ, +3s if ACE is deployed).
  • Get 3 correct answers to drain Shadow AI HP and clear the floor.
  • Consecutive correct answers build a streak multiplier — each hit deals more damage.
  • Wrong answer or timeout = HP damage to you only. AI answers never hurt you.
  • Floor clear = +15% HP restored.

⚡ SECTOR EVENTS — Every 5 Floors

  • 🔒 LOCKDOWN: Characters from the previous sector are banned.
  • ⚡ SURGE: Your first correct answer deals double integrity damage.
  • 💨 BLITZ: Timer cut to 6s — but every correct answer restores 5% HP.

Run ends at 0% HP. Post-run breakdown shows total correct, longest streak, fastest answer, and top operative.

TACTICAL BRIEFING

🔥 BURN PROTOCOL: Bypass any square but you dont see who the person is (or FREE with a Skip power-up).

❌ WARNING: Every incorrect guess costs −5 points.

Pick difficulty 1–10. Higher levels grant point multipliers: Lv 1–3: 1x · Lv 4–8: 2x · Lv 9–10: 4x.

🌸 During a Themed Week all grids lock to one universe. Hot Grid dots show pick-rate intel — 🔥 popular, ❄️ rare. Personal bests are tracked per difficulty.

Strikes cost −5s, Burns cost −60s. Clear the Hardcore levels (1 & 2) before time expires for a massive 7x multiplier.

👁️ SHARINGAN TRAINING

A practice mode — instead of searching, you pick from a set of multiple-choice operatives per cell. No scoring or rankings; pure pattern training.

🎴 ANIME POELTL

One global target per day. Scan operatives to compare attributes — GREEN exact match, YELLOW a genuinely shared trait, RED no correlation. Solve it in as few scans as possible; past days live in the Archives.

☠️ THE GAUNTLET — SURVIVAL TIME ATTACK

5:00 on the clock. Grids grow from 2x2 up to 5x5. Correct guesses add +2s; clearing a board grants a big bonus. Strikes cost −5s, Burns cost −15s.

POWER-UPS — between grids choose one: 🔥 Skip Protocol (free burn), ❄️ Time Freeze (+15s), 🎯 Intel Drop (one cell auto-answered).

TIERS: RECRUIT → OPERATIVE → SHADOW AGENT → PHANTOM → CIPHER GOD.

⚔️ T7P TOWER — ASCENT

Draft a 10-operative squad (2 Elites/30P · 4 Veterans/20P · 4 Recruits/10P) and scale 10 floors. Each floor you deploy one operative — permanently consumed — and a reserve card refills the slot.

CLASH PHASE: name operatives matching the displayed tag before SHADOW.AI does — first to the required hits wins. A single floor loss ends the run (Sudden Death).

♾️ T7P TOWER — SURVIVAL (THE MAINFRAME)

Draft 3 ACE (30P) · 3 CAPTAIN (20P) · 3 CREW (10P). Floors are endless — survive as long as you can. Turns alternate; land 3 correct answers to clear a floor.

CLASSES: ACE +3s timer · CAPTAIN extra cooldown reduction · CREW zero cooldown. Streaks build a damage multiplier. Floor clear restores +15% HP; run ends at 0%.

SECTOR EVENTS (every 5 floors): 🔒 Lockdown (last sector's characters banned), ⚡ Surge (first hit double damage), 💨 Blitz (6s timer, +5% HP per hit).

🕵️ DETECTIVE MODE

One daily mystery operative. Spend attempts to unlock clue cards (series, era, role, combat, hair, persona, studio…) or guess outright. You start at 100 and lose points per attempt — solve it cold for the high score. A team hint unlocks after 6 attempts.

🗂️ BLIND TIER LIST

Ten operatives from one hidden category — a series or a shared trait — dealt one at a time. Drop each into the S/A/B/C pyramid before you see the next. Slots lock when full; reveal the category whenever you like, then share your board.

🧢 ANIME GM

Sign five operatives and split 100% screen time between them. A live radar grades POW/SPD/TEC/INT/SPR, squad synergy and title odds. Hard Mode caps you at OVR 80. Submit to scout the globe and transmit your team rating.

💦 TIER TARGET SPLASH

Place 10 dealt operatives onto multiplier squares — S 4× / A 3× / B 2× / C 1× — to build a Round Value that lands in the Green Zone around the Target. Green = big points, Yellow = small, Red costs a life (3 lives). Pass, Wildcards, ⚡ Special Squares, plus a Hard Mode that doubles points and hides numbers.
